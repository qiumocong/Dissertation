\begin{cabstract}
物体几何特征检测在三维重建、物体识别和机器人操作中具有基础支撑作用。在机器人抓取规划任务中，准确识别面、边缘与角点等几何特征，能够为接触类型判定、候选抓取生成与稳定性分析提供可靠几何先验，从而缓解传统方法过度依赖面接触所导致的抓取构型单一、灵活性不足和复杂物体适应性差等问题。然而，现有几何特征检测方法在边缘与角点区分上仍存在鲁棒性不足、检测精度受噪声与尺度变化影响明显等局限，制约了其在抓取规划中的进一步应用。

针对上述问题，本文在几何特征检测方面提出神经边缘角点检测网络 NECDNet（Neural Edge and Corner Detection Network）。该网络通过分析点云多尺度邻域内的位置分布与法向分布，为查询点构建判别性特征矩阵，并利用层次化神经网络实现面点、边缘点与角点的同步分类，以提升复杂几何边界下关键特征识别的准确性与稳定性。同时，本文构建了包含 1000 个三维点云样本及对应标注的 ABC-Corner 数据集，为网络训练与性能评测提供了数据基础。

在机器人抓取规划方面，本文提出一种考虑边缘与角点接触的六自由度双指抓取规划算法。该算法以 NECDNet 输出的几何特征区域为输入，设计特征化接触检测器搜索对偶接触点，并引入软接触模型进行稳定性评估与碰撞筛选，从而有效提升非规则物体抓取的可行性与抓取构型多样性。相较于仅依赖面接触的规划策略，所提方法能够生成更丰富的接触组合，增强抓取规划在复杂多面体与非对称物体上的适应能力。

实验结果表明，NECDNet 在角点检测任务上的 F1 分数达到 0.815，优于现有方法；所提抓取规划算法在分布复杂度指标（STD、Range、MAD）上均优于 Grasp Pose Detection（GPD）、GraspIt 和 WRS 等方法，抓取构型灵活性显著提高。真实机器人实验进一步验证了角-角、角-边、角-面、边-边、边-面和面-面六类接触组合的有效性与鲁棒性，体现了抓取规划性能与抓取成功率的同步提升。
\end{cabstract}

\ckeywords{点云处理, 几何特征检测, 神经网络, 复杂接触, 机器人抓取规划, 二指平行手爪}

\begin{eabstract}
Geometric feature detection in point clouds is fundamental to 3D reconstruction, object recognition, and robotic manipulation. In robotic grasp planning, accurate detection of surfaces, edges, and corners provides reliable geometric priors for contact-type determination, grasp candidate generation, and stability analysis, thereby alleviating limitations of face-only strategies, such as low configuration diversity, poor flexibility, and weak adaptability to complex objects. However, existing geometric feature detection methods still suffer from limited robustness in edge-corner discrimination and sensitivity to noise and scale variations, which restricts their downstream effectiveness in grasp planning.

To address these issues, this thesis proposes NECDNet (Neural Edge and Corner Detection Network) for geometric feature detection. NECDNet builds discriminative feature matrices by analyzing positional and normal distributions within multi-scale local neighborhoods, and employs a hierarchical neural architecture to jointly classify surface, edge, and corner points, improving feature recognition accuracy and stability under complex geometric boundaries. In addition, the ABC-Corner dataset with 1,000 labeled 3D point-cloud samples is constructed to support network training and evaluation.

For robotic grasp planning, this thesis further proposes a six-degree-of-freedom two-finger grasp planning algorithm that explicitly considers edge and corner contacts. Using geometric feature regions extracted by NECDNet, the algorithm designs feature-oriented contact detectors to search for antipodal contact points, and introduces a soft-contact model for stability evaluation and collision filtering. Compared with conventional face-dominant planning strategies, the proposed method generates richer contact combinations and improves adaptability to irregular objects, including complex polyhedral and asymmetric shapes.

Experimental results show that NECDNet achieves an F1 score of 0.815 in corner detection, outperforming existing methods. The proposed grasp planning algorithm also outperforms Grasp Pose Detection (GPD), GraspIt, and WRS on distribution complexity metrics (STD, Range, and MAD), indicating significantly enhanced configuration flexibility. Real-robot experiments further validate the effectiveness and robustness of six contact combinations, including corner-corner, corner-edge, corner-face, edge-edge, edge-face, and face-face, demonstrating simultaneous gains in grasp planning performance and grasp success rate.
\end{eabstract}
