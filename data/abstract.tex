\begin{cabstract}
针对物体几何特征检测精度不足与机器人抓取规划灵活性受限的问题，本文提出一套融合神经网络特征学习与基于模型抓取规划的方法框架。首先，提出基于多维特征融合的点云边缘与角点检测网络 NECDNet（Neural Edge and Corner Detection Network）。该网络通过分析点云多尺度邻域内的位置分布与法向分布构建特征矩阵，并利用层次化神经网络实现面点、边缘点与角点的同步分类；同时，构建包含 1000 个模型的 ABC-Corner 数据集用于网络训练。其次，提出一种考虑边缘与角点接触的六自由度双指抓取规划算法。该算法利用 NECDNet 提取特征区域，设计特征化接触检测器搜索对偶接触点，并引入软接触模型进行稳定性评估，有效提升了非规则物体抓取的可行性与抓取构型多样性。实验结果表明，NECDNet 的角点检测 F1 分数达到 0.815，优于现有方法；所提抓取规划算法在分布复杂度指标（STD、Range、MAD）上均优于 Grasp Pose Detection（GPD）、GraspIt 等方法，抓取构型灵活性显著提升。机器人实验进一步验证了角-角、角-边等六类接触组合的有效性与鲁棒性，显示出抓取规划性能与抓取成功率的同步提升。
\end{cabstract}

\ckeywords{点云处理, 几何特征检测, 神经网络, 机器人抓取规划, 二指平行手爪}

\begin{eabstract}
To address the limited accuracy of object geometric feature detection and the restricted flexibility of robotic grasp planning, this thesis proposes a framework that combines neural-network feature learning with model-based robotic grasp planning. First, a multi-feature fusion point-cloud edge and corner detection network, NECDNet (Neural Edge and Corner Detection Network), is developed. NECDNet builds feature matrices from positional and normal distributions in multi-scale neighborhoods, and uses a hierarchical neural architecture to jointly classify faces, edges, and corners for geometric feature extraction. In addition, the ABC-Corner dataset with 1,000 models is constructed for network training. Second, a six-degree-of-freedom two-finger grasp planning algorithm considering edge and corner contacts is proposed. Based on feature regions extracted by NECDNet, the algorithm designs feature-aware contact detectors to search for antipodal contact pairs, and introduces a soft-contact model for stability evaluation. This effectively addresses grasping challenges for irregular objects and improves the diversity of grasp planning. Experiments show that NECDNet achieves a corner-detection F1 score of 0.815, outperforming existing methods. The proposed grasp planning algorithm also outperforms model-based methods such as Grasp Pose Detection (GPD) and GraspIt on distribution complexity metrics (STD, Range, and MAD), with a significant gain in grasp configuration flexibility. Robotic experiments further verify the effectiveness and robustness of six contact combinations, including corner-corner and corner-edge contacts, demonstrating improved grasp planning performance and grasp success rate.
\end{eabstract}

\ekeywords{Point cloud processing, Geometric feature detection, Neural network, Robotic grasp planning, Two-finger parallel gripper}
